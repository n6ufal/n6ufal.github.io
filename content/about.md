Hi, I'm Alif Naufal.

I'm an English Literature graduate from Indonesia. I spend an unreasonable amount of time writing to myself, listening to music, watching movies, hanging out with my girlfriend, and falling into internet rabbit holes.

This site is my personal corner of the internet and occasional notebook.

Most of the posts here start as a note, a question, or an idea I want to think through. Sometimes it turns into an essay. Sometimes it becomes a project. Sometimes it's just me trying to make sense of something.

Topics change constantly. Expect philosophy, technology, writing, movies, music, internet culture, and occasional experiments.

Everything here is written by me. No AI-generated posts.

New here? These are good entry points:

- [Hello World!](/posts/hello-world/) — why this site exists
- [Amor Fati](/posts/amor-fati/) — Stoicism and accepting what you can't control
- [What Happens When You Take a Word Game Too Seriously](/posts/what-happens-when-you-take-a-word-game-too-seriously/) — building Letter Demon, my first project.
