About

1 min · Alif Naufal

Hi, I’m Alif Naufal (n6ufal), based in Indonesia. I studied English Literature, so stories and language shape how I see the world — but I spend just as much time lost in music. Lately that’s meant post-hardcore, grunge, jerk, dream pop, shoegaze, progressive house, and Midwest emo. I’m also a massive movie nerd.

Ideas come and go too fast, so I created HEADSPACE, a dumping ground for notes, half-formed thoughts, and 3 AM nonsense in English, Indonesian, or sleep-deprived babble. I’m teaching myself Python, HTML, and Markdown, trying to build things and become a better writer. Lately I’ve been fixated on UAPs, evolutionary biology, fragrance chemistry, and the Breaking Bad universe.
